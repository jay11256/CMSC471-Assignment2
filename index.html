Contextualizing Outcomes: Substantiated vs. Dismissed Complaints

Transparent Analysis

Outcome Differentiation (1985–2020)

Granular Integrity

  • Substantiated (Solid): Complaints where the CCRB found sufficient evidence of misconduct.
  • Dismissed (Dashed): Includes "Unsubstantiated" (insufficient evidence) and "Exonerated" (incident occurred but was lawful).
  • Growth Gap: Highlights how most complaints do not result in a misconduct finding.

Why this Matters

Transparent visualization distinguishes between accusations and findings. By separating these outcomes, we see that while complaint volume may be high, the actual findings of misconduct follow a different trajectory.

Manipulated Perspective

Ethnic Distribution of Allegations

Absolute Volume Focus

  • Total Misconduct Events: This chart aggregates all recorded allegations to show the total volume.
  • Ethnic Disparity: Ranks demographics by absolute count to emphasize which groups generate the most records.

Black Hat Techniques

Intentional Conflation: This chart purposefully ignores whether a complaint was substantiated or dismissed. By grouping all accusations together, it inflates the perceived frequency of misconduct and masks the fact that many entries were exonerated.
